About us

A small team in Pannipitiya that plans pilgrimages, family holidays and group tours, and prepares the visas that go with them.

How we work

Most of our travellers come to us through their temple, their workplace or a friend who has travelled with us before. Many are going abroad for the first time, so we take care of the details: the route, the hotels, the coaches and guides, travel insurance and the visa forms.

Before every group leaves, our office checks that each traveller's visa, insurance and health declarations are in order. On the road, a tour leader travels with the group and stays in touch with our office.

Why we built this website

Until now our bookings lived in spreadsheets, e-mail threads and paper files. This site lets you book a tour, pay your invoice and follow your visa application in one place - and lets our staff see the full status of every trip.
